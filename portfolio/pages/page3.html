Is It Just Food?

Is it just food explores the complexities of family communication through food. Growing up, as many others, communication between the 5 of my sisters and our mother has been challenging and as so many others food has always been my mom's constant love language. Is it just food is a series of 5 1-minute videos with each of my sisters narrating a story or a memory about a food item from our childhood.
