When Shame Became Pride

When Shame became Pride was created to confront my own passivity and explore how easily people can become political puppets in the face of crisis. The work consists of a five-channel video installation, displayed horizontally across five iPads. By placing myself on screen alongside the Pink Panther puppet, I explore the tension between being an informed witness and a silent spectator. The puppet imagery serves as a metaphor for the way news and media can reduce us to inactive observers, turning real-world tragedies into a series of “bits” or “running gags” that we watch from a distance.

Year: 2025

Medium: Video
