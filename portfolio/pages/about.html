About Me

I am an Egyptian visual artist and designer based in Richmond, Virginia/ Doha, Qatar. My work explores themes of identity, memory, and the human experience through a variety of mediums including video, installation, and graphic design. I am passionate about creating immersive and thought-provoking experiences that challenge viewers to engage with the world around them in new ways.

Bachelors degree in Graphic Design, VCUQ, class of 2025

MFA in Visual Communication Candidate, VCU, class of 2027
